MoonBroom Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

MoonBroom ("the game") is made by parpsyche ("we"). This policy explains what data the game handles and why. Contact: parthsachdeva14@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We do not run any servers, and nothing is sent to us. The game has no accounts of its own.
  • Your game is saved on your phone. If you sign in to Google Play Games, a copy is also saved in your Google account.
  • Optional video ads come from Google AdMob, which uses your advertising ID.
  • Purchases go through Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • Co-op sends some game data straight to the other players' phones on your Wi-Fi. It does not go through the internet.
  • The game has no analytics or crash reporting of its own. Unity's analytics and crash reporting are not included in the game.

What the game stores on your phone

The game keeps these on your phone:

  • your saved games
  • your settings
  • which items you have bought
  • which achievements you have unlocked
  • a random ID made when the game is first run (see "Co-op" below)
  • the address of the last co-op host you joined

Earlier test versions (0.3.0 and before) used your phone's Android device ID to identify you in co-op. The game now reads that ID only on your phone, to find your progress in saves made by those versions. It is never sent anywhere.

Android backup. If Android backup is on, Android may copy the game's saves and settings to your Google account and restore them when you reinstall. You can turn this off in your phone's backup settings.

Google Play Games (Android only, optional)

On Android the game can sign you in to Google Play Games automatically. The game works the same without it. When you are signed in, Google receives:

  • your Play Games player identity (gamer name and avatar)
  • the achievements you unlock
  • a copy of your solo game (Saved Games), so you can restore it on a new or reinstalled phone

If you have hosted co-op, your solo game also holds the crew names and random IDs of the players who joined you, so those go into the copy too.

Google stores this data in your Google account. We cannot see it. You control your Play Games profile and who can see it. You can delete your Play Games data from your Play Games profile (https://play.google.com/games/profile) or your Google Account. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Advertising (Android and iOS only)

The mobile versions show rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. An ad plays only when you choose to watch one for a bonus. AdMob may collect and process:

  • your phone's advertising ID
  • your IP address and the approximate location it gives
  • information about your phone and the app, and how you interact with ads

AdMob uses this data to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Ads may be personalised based on this data.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form before any ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or limit ad personalisation, in your phone's settings. See how Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Buying the Moon Pass or the Crew Pass removes the videos. The macOS desktop edition has no ads.

Purchases (Android and iOS only)

In-app purchases go through Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details. The store gives the game a receipt for each purchase, so it can unlock the item. The receipt says which item was bought, when, and the order number. It does not include your name, email address or payment details.

Buying while in a co-op crew (Android). If you buy a cash crate, the launch offer or a season pass while you are in someone else's crew, your phone sends the purchase receipt to the host's phone. The host's phone checks the receipt is real and pays the item in that crew. The host's phone keeps a code from the receipt in its save, so the same purchase can't be paid twice in that crew. If the host cannot check it, you get the item when you go back to your own game.

Co-op on the same Wi-Fi

Co-op works only on your local Wi-Fi network. One phone hosts, and the others join by typing the host's local address (for example 192.168.1.20). The phones talk to each other directly. Nothing goes through the internet or to us.

When you play co-op:

  • The host's phone receives your crew name, the random ID made when the game was first run, and your moves and game state. Your crew name is "Crew-" followed by four characters taken from that random ID, for example "Crew-3F2A".
  • The other players' phones receive your crew name, your game state and a scrambled form of your random ID. The scrambled form cannot be turned back into the ID. It stays the same each time you play, so a host can recognise you when you rejoin.
  • Each phone sees the other phones' local network addresses. Phones always need these to talk to each other. They are addresses inside your Wi-Fi network, not your internet address.
  • The host's phone saves each player's crew name, random ID, cash and gear in its game. Saves made by older versions may still include the Android device ID of players who joined then.
  • Co-op data is encrypted while it travels over your Wi-Fi network, so other devices on the network cannot read it.

There is no chat, and players cannot send each other messages, pictures or other content.

Notifications (Android)

If you allow notifications, the game sets up one reminder on your phone for when your robots have filled the hold. The reminder is created on your phone and nothing is sent to us. You can turn it off in Settings > Notifications or in Android's settings.

Permissions

On Android, the game asks for these:

  • internet and network access, for ads, purchases, Play Games and co-op
  • permission to show notifications
  • access to your advertising ID

It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

The game is for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 is using the game, contact us at the address above. We hold no data ourselves, but we can tell you how to reset the advertising data Google holds.

Deleting your data

  • Settings > Reset my data erases your saved games, achievements and the last host address from your phone and starts a new game. Your purchases and the random co-op ID are kept.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes everything it stored on your phone. A backup made by Android may still hold a copy (see "Android backup" above).
  • Saved Games and achievements in Google Play Games are deleted from your Play Games profile or your Google Account (see above).
  • Advertising data: reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, or contact Google.
  • Co-op data on another player's phone is deleted when that player resets their game or uninstalls it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
