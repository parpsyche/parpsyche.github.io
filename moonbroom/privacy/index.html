MoonBroom Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

MoonBroom ("the game") is made by parpsyche ("we"). This policy explains what data the game handles and why. Contact: parthsachdeva14@gmail.com.

What we collect

We do not run any servers and do not collect personal data ourselves. The game has no accounts of its own. Your game progress is saved on your device, and optionally in your Google account as described below.

The game uses third-party services that may process data as described below.

Google Play Games (Android only, optional)

On Android the game can sign you in to Google Play Games automatically. Playing without it works the same. When you are signed in, Google receives:

  • your Play Games player identity (gamer name and avatar)
  • the achievements you unlock
  • a copy of your game progress (Saved Games), so you can restore it on a new or reinstalled phone

This data is stored by Google in your Google account, not by us. You control your Play Games profile and its visibility, and you can delete your Play Games data from your Play Games profile (https://play.google.com/games/profile) or your Google Account. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Android backup

If Android backup is on for your device, Android may include the game's save file in your device backup to your Google account and restore it when you reinstall. You can turn this off in your device's backup settings.

Advertising (Android and iOS only)

The mobile versions show optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier
  • your IP address and the approximate location it implies
  • device and app information, and how you interact with ads

AdMob uses this data to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Ads may be personalised based on this data. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice from the consent form. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or limit ad personalisation, in your device settings. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

The macOS desktop edition contains no ads.

Purchases (Android and iOS only)

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details. We only receive confirmation of which item was bought, so the game can unlock it.

Local multiplayer

When you play co-op on the same Wi-Fi network, the game sends your crew name (for example "Crew-3F2A") and your game state directly to the other players' devices. Nothing is sent to us.

Notifications (Android)

If you allow notifications, the game schedules a reminder on your device for when your robots have finished working. This is local to your device and sends nothing to us.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 is using the game, contact us at the address above. We hold no data ourselves, and we can tell you how to reset the advertising data held by Google.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes all game data stored on your device. Saved Games and achievements in Google Play Games are deleted from your Play Games profile or Google Account (see above). To reset advertising data, use your device's advertising ID settings or contact Google.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
